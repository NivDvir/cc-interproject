# Dispatcher queue — cross-project gaps

This file is the backlog for problems found during cross-project contact that are not any one
project's to fix: a stale reference in another project's tree, a head that cannot be reached, a
remit nobody has recorded, an unconfirmed convergence. Log it here with enough context for someone
with no other context to act on it cold, then move on — it is not the finder's job to fix, and not
every gap needs to reach the person these projects serve.
